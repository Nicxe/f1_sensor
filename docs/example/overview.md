See how other people use F1 Sensor. These community projects have their own hardware, dependencies and maintenance; the bundled [dashboard cards](/cards/cards-overview) are the quickest starting point for most installations.

## A race schedule on your desk

![F1 Pit Wall e-ink display](/img/F1_pitwall.png)

[F1 Pit Wall](/example/e-ink) uses ESPHome and an e-ink display to show the next race weekend through Home Assistant.

## A dashboard built around your style

- [Tiidler’s custom dashboard](/example/custom-card) combines schedule, standings and weather in a personal layout.
- [@boredmthfkr’s standings cards](/example/custom-card-by) include community YAML for custom:button-card, with the original attribution and setup notes.

## Share your setup

Post your dashboard in [Show and tell](https://github.com/Nicxe/f1_sensor/discussions/categories/show-and-tell). Include the installed version, any extra cards or helpers, and enough context for someone else to reproduce it.
